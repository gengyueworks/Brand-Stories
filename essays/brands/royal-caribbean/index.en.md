# 🚢 "Perfect Day at Mexico" Unveiled in New York: How Royal Caribbean is Reshaping the Future of Vacationing

*Geng Yue · Brand Stories*

---

Royal Caribbean, the world's largest cruise brand, recently unveiled its grandest and most groundbreaking destination project yet in New York—"Perfect Day at Mexico."

Located next to the port of Costa Maya, Mexico, the island spans over 200 acres (approximately 81 hectares) and is scheduled to open in September 2027, with a designed capacity of around 20,000 guests.

As the newest addition to the "Perfect Day" series, it is regarded by Royal Caribbean as the "crown jewel" and became the absolute protagonist of this global press launch.

![](images/image1.jpeg)

I had the privilege of attending the event at Pier 59 in Manhattan, New York, joining travel industry partners from around the world to witness Royal Caribbean's future vision for this island.

After hearing about their comprehensive design plans, my expectations for this island were completely set.

It is not only the grandest and most groundbreaking vacation project in Royal Caribbean's history,

but it also redefines the industry standard for the "future of island vacations."

![](images/image2.jpeg)

> "Over the past 50 years, we have consistently shattered stereotypes about cruise travel, transforming into a global vacation brand for all generations.
> 
> 'Perfect Day at Mexico' not only continues our spirit of creativity but is a systematic reshaping of the destination experience."

Said Michael Bayley, President and CEO of Royal Caribbean International, at the venue.

Why is this destination so different?

How will it manage to be both dreamlike and concrete, festive and thoughtful?

**A Giant Park:**

**An Immersive Kingdom Built for All Generations of Travelers**

Unlike traditional cruise ports, "Perfect Day at Mexico" is designed as an all-weather, immersive, multi-themed amusement-level experience system.
It is divided into seven themed neighborhoods, covering everything from thrilling adventures, lazy river drifting, family sharing, and adult-only sanctuaries, to local culture immersion.

![](images/image5.jpeg)

**A Bold Transformation**

The site where "Perfect Day at Mexico" is situated was originally the port of Costa Maya along Mexico's Caribbean coast.

Royal Caribbean purchased an exclusive stretch of coastline adjacent to the Costa Maya port. This is not a lease or a partnership;

they purchased this coastline outright, solely to transform it into an all-immersive destination exclusive to Royal Caribbean guests.

Other cruise lines can still dock at Costa Maya, but they cannot step foot onto this upcoming holiday island—its entrance is open only to those travelers who turn dreams into reality.

![](images/image6.jpeg)

**CocoCay Experience Upgraded**

This is an ultra-giant holiday park—not just because it has many thrilling rides, but because of its three-in-one integration of "all-age coverage + high-density entertainment + scenic experience."
This project will further expand Royal Caribbean's destination portfolio, echoing the existing "Perfect Day at CocoCay" from south to north, forming a dual-core Caribbean layout.

They did not just bring everything that succeeded at CocoCay; they also added many elements never seen before,

creating a fully upgraded, absolutely magical water kingdom.
The excitement is being unveiled.

1. **Loco Waterpark**

An energy-packed zone built for water enthusiasts.

![](images/image8.jpeg)

Starting with "Jaguar's Peak," which is currently the tallest slide tower in North and South America, standing at approximately 170 feet (about 52 meters) high and topped with a jaguar sculpture.

Its main slide runs for 2 minutes and 15 seconds—a duration rarely seen in water slides, where ordinary slides usually end in a matter of seconds.

![](images/image9.gif)

During these two minutes, you will twist, drift, and leap, experiencing the ultimate rush of an accelerating heartbeat.

> "We want to give them a bit of a scare, not too much of course, but we want every kid to walk into Jaguar's Peak with a little bit of nervousness and a lot of excitement—it will absolutely become one of the wildest memories of their lives."
> 
> —Michael Bayley

![](images/image10.jpeg)

Inspired by the traditional Mexican sombrero, the slide winds rapidly around the brim of the hat before splashing into a pool shaped like a margarita glass.

**North America's Tallest Family Raft Slide:**

Accommodates up to six people launching together, perfect for families to challenge the high-speed waves side-by-side.

**Interactive Viewing Slides:**

All slides and viewing platforms are intricately woven together, allowing for great photos of the launch and making it easy to watch the entire slide process, serving as the perfect stage for sharing photos and cheering.

**Splash Cove**

**Splashaway Bay**

**A Water Kingdom for Kids, a Sanctuary of Peace for Parents**

![](images/image23.jpeg)

This is the largest Splashaway Bay in Royal Caribbean's history,

creating a water castle dedicated to children under 6 years old.

![](images/image24.jpeg)

Bubble pools, interactive water walls, mini slide towers, and spray nozzles—this is their main stage for running, screaming, splashing, and playing.

The truly brilliant part of this area is that it also considers the parents' perspective.
It is not about "handing the kids over to the water," but giving parents places to sit, drinks to enjoy, and shade, while keeping a clear line of sight to see where their child slides down and lands.

![](images/image25.jpeg)

**Splash Summit**

Serving as a transition zone, it offers higher-level challenges for older children, with elevated slides that still maintain an open, visible design.
The entire area is organized around "space separation + visual connection," with soft flooring, shaded pathways, and fully optimized shower and transit channels.

![](images/image26.gif)

Jay Schneider said at the venue:

> "One thing we learned from CocoCay is that kids can go down the slide 100 times, but parents just want a place where they can watch."

Thus, the design of this entire family area comes down to a single principle: you don't have to slide with them, but you can always keep them in sight.

**Crazy Lazy River**

**A Floating Living Room on Water**

![](images/image27.jpeg)

This will be the **longest lazy river in the world**, with the entire drift expected to take 54 minutes.

> But "you can get out at any time, or you can soak in it all day.
> 
> It allows you to float easily with a tequila or your favorite drink in hand."

Guests can enter the river with drinks and sip as they drift.

Even better, several "Oasis Bars" are set along the river. You can paddle ashore at any time to enter a tavern with live music and a party atmosphere, finish a drink, and continue drifting.

> "If you want, you can spend the entire day in this river."

Additionally, they have set up a "Crazy Zone" branch line—where the current suddenly accelerates and generates waves, perfect for thrill-seekers.
Outside the main channel, multiple entrances and exits connect seamlessly with the Cabanas, making it possible to "drift out of your cabin and drift back in."

This is a water pathway where you can truly "spend the whole day," which Royal Caribbean calls a "**Floating Lounge.**"

2. **Chill Island:**

**North and South Leisure Beaches: The Right to Do Nothing**

Chill Island is the longest stretch of natural beach on "Perfect Day at Mexico."
It features a double-beach structure on the north and south, embracing the entire front of the island with a total length of nearly 2 miles.

There are no checklist tasks and no tourist routes.

This is a dedicated zone for "doing absolutely nothing."

![](images/image29.jpeg)

The North Beach is perfect for chatting, walking around, and listening to music.

The South Beach is quieter, ideal for playing in the sand with kids or staring at the sea alone.

The entire beach is equipped with plenty of free beach chairs, water stations, snack shacks, umbrellas, and soft sand.

All you need is a towel, a drink, and an afternoon with no plans.

This is a space designed for letting go, where every second can slow down.

![](images/image30.gif)

Jay Schneider said at the venue:

> "Chill means something different to every guest. Some want to listen to Caribbean music, some want to soak by the water, and some just want their feet in the white sand, holding a drink and letting their mind wander."

And here, every definition of "Chill" is taken seriously.

3. **Costa Beach Club:**

**A Quieter, More Premium Luxury Private Island**

If Chill Island is a white sand coast for "free drifting,"

then Costa Beach Club is another way to experience "luxury immersive relaxation."

This is the only premium quiet zone on the island that requires an entry fee,

offering a serene space tailored for high-net-worth travelers.

Here, there are no loud parties or kids running around.

Instead, it offers a nearly hidden, high-quality relaxation experience:

A heated infinity pool: Facing the Caribbean Sea, water and sky merging seamlessly without boundaries.
Fine dining restaurant: Complete with a waiter ordering system, table service, and drinks on demand.
Next-generation Cabanas:
Equipped with private refrigeration, butler service, and private bathrooms.
Private decks, umbrellas, and double daybeds—everything designed for relaxation.
Some feature private plunge pools, allowing guests to enjoy the coastal views without ever leaving their Cabana.

This is the highest expression of "Chill."

Not for socializing, but simply to be taken care of. It is the ideal sanctuary for travelers who truly understand how to enjoy the "right to quietness," representing an elegant, unpretentious lifestyle.

![](images/image31.jpeg)

4. **El Hideaway**

**An 18+ Adult-Only Party Cove**

If Costa is the island's "vein,"

then El Hideaway is its "artery."

This is a party cove designed exclusively for adults aged 18 and older,

completely isolated from the noise and distraction of children.

Here, you can choose to jump into the water to let loose and forget everything,

or gather with friends to celebrate a birthday, deciding exactly how you want to play.

A massive infinity pool: Featuring a swim-up bar and a shallow water dance floor, perfect for drinking and dancing.
Live music and resident DJs: The beat never stops, even after the sun goes down.
The Ultimate Party Cabana: Accommodates up to 20 people, equipped with a private pool, ice buckets, a sound system, and exclusive access, tailored for birthdays, bachelor/bachelorette parties, and other celebrations.
All-day bar service: Ready for a toast and a gathering at any moment.

As Jay Schneider remarked at the venue:

> "It is loud, it is wild, it is not for everyone—and that is exactly the point of it."

This is a kingdom of freedom belonging to adults,

and the place where your own rhythm is truly set free.

**Interview | An Interview with Angie Stephen, Senior Vice President of Royal Caribbean**

**Connecting Three Generations of Travel Dreams with a Single Ship**

At the "Perfect Day at Mexico" launch, we had the privilege of interviewing Angie Stephen, Senior Vice President of International Business for Royal Caribbean International.
When discussing the design philosophy behind "Perfect Day at Mexico," her tone flowed with determination, passion, and brilliance.

And you will quickly be infected and moved by the exciting future she describes for the new products.

![](images/image47.jpeg)

Having been based in Shanghai and Singapore, she has a deep understanding of the Asian market.

**Q1. Could you share some of the significant differences in service and destinations across different global markets for Royal Caribbean? Especially regarding the needs of Asian travelers?**

**A (Angie):**

We have observed that Asian travelers tend to prefer "the biggest, the newest, and the most unique" experiences when choosing their trips.

In recent years, the Asian market has also shown a shift from large tour groups to smaller groups and independent travel.

They particularly love the Royal Caribbean cruise experience, not just because the ship design and facilities are outstanding, but also because they can experience a truly international atmosphere on board, such as diverse international dining, highly shareable visual scenes for social media, and rich entertainment experiences.

Many Asian travelers choose to depart from Shanghai to Japan, or from Singapore to close-destination itineraries like Malaysia and Thailand.

Now, more and more people are also starting to try long-haul cruise vacations from Asia to Europe or the Caribbean.

![](images/image48.gif)

When discussing Chinese tourists, especially the demand for "multi-generational travel" and "families spending vacations together," she did not hide her understanding and expectations.
She shared with us how Royal Caribbean gains insights into the diverse needs of Chinese travelers, continuously optimizing products and services, and how the "family-friendly" vacation concept makes travel with three generations possible.

**Q2. What specific products or strategic adjustments has Royal Caribbean made for the Chinese market or Chinese travelers?**

**A:**

We found that Chinese travelers particularly tend to travel as a family unit, especially the "three-generation travel" model, which is very common: children, parents, and grandparents traveling together.

To support this, we have developed a complete system to ensure that every generation can find fun based on their own interests.

For instance, our "Adventure Ocean" kids club combines entertainment with education, staying open all day and being completely free. Children can play to their hearts' content here, while parents can enjoy dinner, karaoke, or theater performances with peace of mind.

**Q3. Chinese travelers often want to deeply explore destinations while also desiring private spaces. How does Royal Caribbean respond to this demand for "having both"?**

**A:**

This is exactly an aspect we value very much.

Royal Caribbean has always emphasized the concept of "satisfying every traveler." Therefore, we not only provide rich and diverse public experiences, but we also set up "ship-within-a-ship" exclusive areas on board—such as Suite Class.

This area features a private deck, exclusive lounge, customized dining, and even services from a Royal Genie.

Royal Genies are more than just butlers; they are dream coordinators who can arrange a full suite of personalized services for guests from embarkation to disembarkation.

If you want to explore deeply while retaining your own sense of ceremony and private space, our suite system balances the two perfectly.

The dress code that day was "Caribbean Pirates," and attendees dressed up for the occasion.

She wore a blue blazer with navy patterns, echoing the maritime atmosphere of the "Caribbean Night of Dreams."
I dressed in a maritime style, with gold hair accessories and a nautical silk scarf, looking like two travelers from different routes meeting briefly on a ship.

What left a deep impression on me was a phrase Angie said naturally—

> "It's really about making dreams happen."

At that moment of resonance, we both smiled—fellow dreamers.

On Asian itineraries, we specifically provide Chinese language services, Asian-flavored dining, and localized entertainment content, ensuring that travelers can experience familiar cultural touches while enjoying international standards.

We have found that Chinese travelers possess strong curiosity and a desire to explore. They are eager to try the latest and largest products, and they place great value on detailed experiences and family rituals during the journey.

**A:**
Yes, I lived and worked in Shanghai for a year and a half, which was my experience before relocating to Singapore.

Asia, especially China, is a market I am incredibly passionate about. It is full of vitality, warmth, and boundless potential.

I hope for more collaboration and exchanges in the future, and you are most welcome to share our stories with more Chinese travelers and families.
