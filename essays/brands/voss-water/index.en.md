*Geng Yue · Brand Stories*

When you encounter the word "**purity**," what comes to mind?

***05,***

***IN THIS LIFE YOU GET OUT WHAT YOU PUT IN.***

Keep your inputs pure at all times

Years ago, a remark by Jonathan Lee shook me. The gist was: "In this age, most content is garbage; what has value is very little. If you keep feeding swill to your audience, your audience will turn into swine."

So I have always been meticulous about the quality of my inputs — choosing books, music, and films of the highest caliber. Only then might I produce writing that doesn't waste the reader's time, even if it's only a phrase or two.

Life works the same way. YOU ARE WHAT YOU EAT. What you consume is what you become. I trust this more and more.

![Purity of input](images/046.jpg)

I used to be someone who couldn't drink water on schedule. Sometimes I'd be so busy with photography that my mouth would go dry for hours, yet I wouldn't pause for a proper sip.

Now, drinking water has become something I look forward to every day. It feels light, refreshing. I sense that even my cells have grown softer, more alive. It creates a curious interval of connection with myself.

![Looking forward to water](images/047.jpg)

This winter, every day I see news that leaves me stunned and dizzy. And I think — what is truly important? To do anything well, to care for anyone, you must first care for yourself. Live well.

**Be kind to yourself. Drink water, and drink good water. Make drinking water something with quality and texture.** I am immersed in this loveliness every day.

![Drink good water](images/048.jpg)

The ceramic artist Kawai Kanjiro once said: "When you buy something, you buy yourself."

Choosing a bottle of water is, in fact, an expression of your aesthetic. Like VOSS — with inner quality and outer style, accomplished on both fronts.

A quick note: VOSS is soft water. Water comes in varying degrees of hardness, and soft water is gentler on the body and more readily absorbed. Sourced from Norway's deep artesian flow, free of any trace of pollution or impurity, it ensures your inputs remain pure at every moment.
