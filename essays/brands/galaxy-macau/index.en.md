Let me first share some **Macau travel tips** from a travel blogger's perspective.

1. Visit Macau's most famous landmark, the Ruins of St. Paul's.

2. Wander through Senado Square and soak in the century-old Portuguese colonial architecture.

3. Experience the world's highest bungee jump at the 223-meter Macau Tower.

4. Stroll the candy-colored streets of Coloane Village.

5. Explore the charming pastel-green Taipa Houses-Museum, a classic example of Portuguese-style architecture.

6. Stop by the bright yellow St. Dominic's Church and square.

![Macau attractions](images/006.jpg)

![Macau attractions](images/007.jpg)

7. Visit the Macau Museum for a panoramic view overlooking the Ruins of St. Paul's.

8. See the golden Lotus Square, built to commemorate Macau's handover.

9. Head to Guia Fortress and Lighthouse for a sweeping city panorama.

10. Pay a visit to the historic A-Ma Temple, where incense has burned for centuries.

11. Take a romantic stroll with your loved one along Love Lane.

12. Discover the colorful, tucked-away alleyways of Taipa Village and their hidden stories.
