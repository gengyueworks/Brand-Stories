# What Hidden-Gem Independent Magazines Would You Recommend?

*Geng Yue · Brand Stories · 2026-06-18*

---

In the digital age, there's a print magazine that records UOOYAA GIRLS — free, real, adventurous — and the new ways people are telling stories in this era. From the weekend until today, I've been quietly turning its pages, one by one.

I used to be a magazine editor. My job was telling stories with images. Seeing this magazine felt magical — it's like an intertextuality with that "magazine era."

As if you've been drifting outside for a long time, and suddenly a spaceship flies over, opens up, and inside is everything shiny you collected years ago. Wild ideas, every kind of aesthetic — all of it is in there. **Her name is "UOOYAA ZINE."** After finishing it, one feeling remained: **I never thought something like this still existed in this era.**

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-0b896118215b87d5f7260506dce2c475_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

The 16th issue I'm reading now is the newest one.

It opens with a photo story called **CARNIVAL BY THE LAKE.**

### Carnival by the Lake

It captures a fleeting moment of summer —

A group of girls temporarily step off the tracks of everyday life, head to the lake with friends, to explore and play freely.

![img](https://pica.zhimg.com/80/v2-504a633c7f78debb06e66f1c8f2d0208_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Their playfulness is sparked by the wide-open outdoors, centered on the innocent, fearless "wild child" — searching for their own playground in nature.

![img](https://pica.zhimg.com/80/v2-ad636b27a5a2f6c1f56acdf93a97baa9_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Summer, dawn redwoods, sky reflected on the water. Their playfulness awakens in the vast outdoors.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-25d760b311c64b45eeaa247a7f9ae9bb_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

> The more focused and anchored people become, the more they lose their capacity for imagination and play.

May you carry that primal impulse and free imagination, and open a brief "carnival" of your own.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-dd8f61cc24bc0ec11f606f8c1c80f5a1_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

### The Inspiration Universe of Yuka Mizuhara

Japanese rising musician **Yuka Mizuhara** invited the magazine into her Tokyo home to explore the world of her creativity; she was the "last disciple" of famed musician TOWA TEI. After working across multiple identities,

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-f3cc950e2fff5a768b09e04871beb5f5_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

she didn't follow the established, typical artist path, but **forged her own creative route as a DJ and musician**, exploring and practicing in the world of vinyl. The magazine walked into her home, photographed the strange instruments she's collected from around the world, and the stories behind her personal objects.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-edb70429eae98af71d943deec82e25ca_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Out of a love for old architecture, she lives in a **ninety-year-old house** in Tokyo.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-7cf58b966ccfcea518290fed439218f5_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

> For Yuka Mizuhara, both the space she lives in and the objects she treasures hide stories that keep unfolding; collecting them is also a record of her ever-expanding world of inspiration.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-8c99cad8cc59d77a67c0e773ab11bd3f_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

I love that description.

A home isn't just where you live — it's also her **constantly expanding universe of inspiration.**

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-c31986816513e714bc4e04e2285ce2da_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

The next story: life's uncertainty often arrives unannounced, suddenly, into the impermanent… The magazine crossed four cities — Shanghai, Chengdu, Tokyo, New York — to meet eight women from different backgrounds,

and talked with them about how, in moments of disorder, they still hold on to that "playful spirit" and look toward the distance with a lighter stance.

![img](https://pic1.zhimg.com/80/v2-cbe1879af9872b0dc0763d9ba9c450ae_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

> "Is 'chaos' really that bad? **The truest, most alive parts of life can never be fully tamed.** Inside what first looks chaotic hides a different kind of vitality."

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-a51feeae26cd56b7aceb9655711206ab_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

And there's American artist Lina Sun Park, composing intuitively with natural objects — quiet magic in her hands. Dutch artist Paul Kooiker builds an alternate world driven by desire and imagination. South African photographer Nico Krijno splices, reactivates, and reconstructs strange new worlds.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-ae2f8a963825cfc1d233ed7b444c11c3_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Issue 16 opens with a wild outdoor photo story that briefly pulls us away from the everyday.

Then comes an artistic kaleidoscope — **full of playfulness and improvisation.**

Breaking rules, away from perfection, embracing the uncontrolled, reconstructing and reactivating — **the imagination and playfulness we are losing.**

## Issue 15: The Meaning of Travel Is Not Arrival

I flip back to Issue 15. The cover is a beautiful Klein blue.

"Blue" is a fascinating color. In Japanese, it means immaturity. **It's the state of still growing —** **like the blue of sky and sea, extending into the distance, keeping the possibility of becoming anything.**

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-e8ed7f50c0691e92e0e71cc35ababfd3_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

First stop, Tokyo.

In a city built of algorithms and grids, two lost girls. They decide to pack up and briefly leave. **This photo series is called "The Crow's Journey."** Following the flight path of a crow, they venture into the wider wilderness. Along the way, shifting states of mind transform into fantastical landscapes, with unexpected encounters.

![img](https://pic1.zhimg.com/80/v2-b95d13dc60444b1f5f1b24595b56f430_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

The magazine invited eight friends, each opening up the secret corner of the city where they can "escape at a moment's notice." **Travel isn't only about distance — it can also turn inward.**

> These small sanctuaries offer breathing room, letting you step back and savor life's brief pauses.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-8f2150af6dca8f12bfdf32cd25d8d323_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

This issue has a romantic photography series — British photographer John Maclean set out on a world journey to visit the homelands of artists who inspired him: to the doorstep of William Eggleston, to the places where Kandinsky lived. **It's like one creator paying tribute to another**, acknowledging the influence of those who came before. **To experience how they grew, then reinterpret and create in your own intuitive way, feeling the ripples.**

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-dbf077cefe3f2d70ef61e4e319411cd6_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Finally, Korean-American photographer Kang-hee Kim. Visa restrictions have kept her from returning to Korea for over a decade, and from traveling outside America. She responds to limits with surreal escapes — **in her art, she layers familiar spaces into new ones, freeing herself from reality's constraints.**

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-19363660e4c765c6fab0d648a222520c_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Create your own unknown inside others' familiar places.

**Travel, sometimes, is imagination taking flight.**

![img](https://pic1.zhimg.com/80/v2-328aab8d35e2f1bab50d0ccf005fa008_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Painter Sean Landers has been drawing one character for over thirty years — Plankboy.

> "Plankboy is both a smoothly sanded plank of wood, out of place in a natural forest, and a middle-aged man burdened by various pains."

His work is tender and sensitive, both fragile and confident.

Landers sees him as a kind of externalization of himself — imperfect, yet still persistently working, creating for audiences now and in the future.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-51f7eb358eb8f0bd4a61b54ae3e6c8e6_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

**UOOYAA ZINE** is a magazine published by a clothing brand, once or twice a year, founded in 2018 — only 16 issues so far. Every issue is refined.

**What first made me love it was its aesthetic intelligence.** **The writing reads like a poem with precise rhythm and breath.** **The perspective flows with a strange texture — curious, playful, pioneering, observant.**

## The Journey Through the Magazine

Now I flip back through. Each issue centers on a big theme, inviting creators from different fields to work from their own unique perspectives and experiences. In 2018, the first issue. Theme: **"Barbarella, Hero of Space."**

![img](https://pic1.zhimg.com/80/v2-c73633bd2cc0ca16c0401d5a1df207e4_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Inspired by the 1968 classic sci-fi film directed by Roger Vadim. The film begins with a failed flight — Barbarella's ship crash-lands in a postmodern city, a strange world where reality and dreams interweave.

> Sequined dresses and slip dresses refract strange multicolored light in the sun, blurring the boundary between reality and dream. **When the glorious music plays, life wanders through the boundless universe, reaching toward the immortality of the unknown — humanity is forever driven by curiosity.**

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-5ee05fe57b3390de7392a038e8edacca_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

**The 1968 French sci-fi film, Jane Fonda's transformation, Kubrick's "2001: A Space Odyssey," the 60s hippie spirit of "love and peace."** The same issue includes a survey of sci-fi cult films: from Alien to The Shining to Flash Gordon, even the Queens soundtrack. Very avant-garde, very cool, very fashionable. Very sharp. It even mentions artificial intelligence.

![img](https://pic1.zhimg.com/80/v2-7189db3327be84eaf7da43e8b8917b3e_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

If you love movies, you'll see related artistic reinterpretations.

In another issue, the magazine invited illustrator Laura Passalacqua to create a series of "Pulp Fiction" covers — Laura dressed the cover characters in UOOYAA's fall/winter collection, telling different stories.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-2a49082cccf57b39ec140d2d44816246_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

## Beijing Youth and A Tale of Two Cities

Issue two: **"Beijing Youth."** Looking back at the old Beijing of the 90s. People back then all looked similar: rockers, petty hooligans, idle youths — everyone living carefree. **"My life has nothing to do with yours. I'll live my own life."** **"In that era, every young person was a forerunner of change."** **Full of grit. That real Beijing spirit that once drew us in.**

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-e258a101415bbabf31525d8a5177eccd_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Issue three arrives on bustling Chinatown streets, showing the chaotic, violent aesthetics of Chinatown's little people,

along with the mystery of cultural clash, set to Quentin's film scores and Bruce Lee's eerie screams.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-13573590e9379ebd2b2c46afcd74aed1_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Issue four came to Chengdu and Chongqing.

> UOOYAA's design is always full of contrast and harmony; conflict and strength are forever UOOYAA's keywords. Refined suits paired with playful monster patterns, traditional neo-Chinese with punk, neo-Chinese front plackets with monster jacquard fabrics… **Both dashing and cute, gorgeous yet streetwise, serious without being solemn, traditional yet futuristic — this is UOOYAA's attitude.**

They don't just shoot clothing; they went deep into Chengdu's youth haunts — a Funk & Disco music club next to Sichuan University, a comic store with over 2,000 original American comics, and EchoBay, a pink-toned electronic music space by the Jialing River.

![img](https://pica.zhimg.com/80/v2-96e4deeaf23749064a1da2c785f42138_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

> People from Sichuan really don't care that much. Nobody tries to become someone else, and they don't care about others' judgment. **Let everyone freely become whoever they want to be.**

**Everything fun and comfortable, they love it all.**

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-c65ce6581d1cd48da5509a70a6558d16_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Here, life slows down. People get to know each other better, and those with shared interests come together to enjoy themselves. **In such a magical atmosphere, UOOYAA found its own magical spirit.**

Issue seven: **"Queen of the Next Stop"** — a tribute to the golden age of Hong Kong pop culture.

> The era of superstars. The era of everyone trying hard. The era of Leslie Cheung, Alan Tam, Jacky Cheung. The era of Anita Mui, Brigitte Lin, Maggie Cheung. Also the era of Stephen Chow and Ng Man-tat.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-321c67e71156101da7fbd4823034461b_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

**An era gone forever.** "We discovered what a wonderful era it was, while back then we thought it was terrible."

![img](https://pic1.zhimg.com/80/v2-f86e525824411f62eff384a1e191978d_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Issue 12, "The Graduate," tries to recapture that special rashness of the "young and green."

Standing at the crossroads, occasionally drifting into a trance-like state, not knowing where you're going.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-647a328b7a9f8eaa44e62e2ad944e754_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

In the same issue, Bruno Ceschel, a publisher and photography educator from Milan, recommended the work of five brilliantly talented new faces in art.

![img](https://pica.zhimg.com/80/v2-cc156110103a1ba7ee5099cc3afd135e_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

## Soft Power

Issue 9 is one I love — the symbiosis of softness and tenacity.

The inspiration comes from a physical phenomenon — "non-Newtonian fluid."

> **Between solid and liquid, both soft and hard, stronger under pressure, softer under gentleness.**

Five senses × interviews with five female artists.

> If you only see the brokenness, it's hard to face this world; if you only see the beauty and embrace it with open arms, you may end up hugging a thorn bush. — Huang Jiaqi

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-65551b890c0c2dca584758e5de071de0_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Liu Yimiao perceives the essence of life through painting.

> Beneath those apricot-like shrewd eyes surveying everything, under sun-kissed honey-toned skin, **she has a soft, calm, delicate and humble heart.**

![img](https://pic1.zhimg.com/80/v2-79acb6974ed837fd3b446935a35f65fe_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

In Zhuang Huijia's view, "soft power" means "softening the heart,"

> The more at ease you make yourself, the freer and more carefree you become.

The corresponding scent blends the feeling of cotton, feathers, and mountain peaks — birds flying past, the world calm and clear.

![img](https://pic1.zhimg.com/80/v2-d0d13704ac186d6fa9a07004f0f4213b_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Issue 11 has a photo series by Danish photographer Simone Steenberg

exploring with sincerity and lightness the growth and aging of women together with her 90-year-old grandmother Eva.

![img](https://pic1.zhimg.com/80/v2-216b743d24fc2f1d5fc0d7bd507ae9dc_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

The grandmother said:

> **Allow herself to become vulnerable and curious, and keep trying new ways to explore life.**

Growing old is not a heavy thing.

Female strength has always been a theme UOOYAA explores. Issue 10: **"Castle in the Sky"** — showing women's power in the world.

> An epic tragedy. An epic grandeur.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-67e05c9148490f76b3bad2b44c54b3b7_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

After long confusion, anger and anxiety, **we need strength, hope, and faith.**

## Do Machines Dream?

An interesting theme — a special feature in Issue 8,

> "Do you think AI can dream?" "If it can be called AI, it must dream. Even if it doesn't dream, it will produce some delusions."

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-819a31c58a01bee91a550ac41d9a158c_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

This was 2022, before ChatGPT existed.

Now it's 2026, and AI really can "dream." The magazine was observing before AI became hot — a vision of the future, a concern for the present.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-7818bfb53666c7e651899299f12e2c78_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Now that it's truly 2026, I realize what this magazine has always upheld — exactly the most essential qualities for humanity facing the AI wave.

What makes people irreplaceable? **A bit of childishness suddenly emerging from routine, unique experiences that touch the heart.** **Untamed playfulness, curiosity that defies convention, a natural boredom with the "optimal solution."**

![img](https://pic1.zhimg.com/80/v2-df2e149524cdf5992926a0c06eee2191_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

**Emotional storytelling created by intuition, preference itself as an attitude, creativity that algorithms cannot define.**

When I open UOOYAA ZINE, look at those visual stories, those artist interviews, there's a strong sense of **Call Back** — that texture of the golden age of print media suddenly returns. The anticipation of waiting for the issue. The smell of ink. The slight resistance of paper when turning pages. The breathing rhythm between images and text.

![img](https://pic1.zhimg.com/80/v2-e3618083cd660e2d16bb3385867aaa35_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

I love its temperament. This magazine reminds me of the print media years.

Remembering those days — concentrating wholeheartedly on things you love, being intentional about the content, lingering over treasures. It makes me time-travel instantly, like the city wanderer in "Midnight in Paris."

![img](https://pic1.zhimg.com/80/v2-6f3108047b4f0f33f3a9fa4ac6a0124a_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

The word for homesickness is nostalgia —

Going everywhere to find inspiration, commissioning visual artist interviews, retouching, layout, art-themed features. It's a luxurious look back.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-bd455ee719912f0c7d546dbb82f94cb8_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

It's truly amazing. I want to share it too — that this era still has such a magazine.

We also believe that what you can achieve is only what your heart truly desires. We trust that the people making this magazine, and my own process of writing about it and recommending it, are the same.

## A Touchable Spiritual Archive

I also looked at the clothing brand behind the magazine, and found many celebrities have worn it. I love the style too. The clothing blends retro, urban, fairy-tale and artistic senses together.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-8ed53adea2027c880c51faafdac5980b_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

UOOYAA was founded in 2014. Its clothing organizes each season's collection by theme and emotion, like a chapter of a story. **It makes the wearer look characterful.** Full of personality, unconventional.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-a66e0d89d33aad7dc4297ae53f6f9c0e_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Over the past decade UOOYAA has done many collaborations — from childhood memories of Disney, Ultraman and EVA, to the artistic heights of Christian Lacroix (French haute couture master) and the Centre Pompidou (Paris's top modern and contemporary art museum), each one the vocabulary of another circle. But the brand centers its female narrative on "youth, strength, truth and adventure," and its protagonists dress unconventionally — **real, interesting, curious, independent, unconventional.**

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-ca10510dafb5fc744c0a67ff06c4a280_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

**ZINE is the most complete carrier of this attitude.** Using print to explore fashionable, humanistic, artistic content, and feeding it back into the daily lives of UOOYAA GIRLS — **page by page, entering a quiet world of carefully constructed and polished visual stories.**

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-ad9cfdb3607a732f45510065149c964c_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Global top-tier brands are all doing content — Loewe is doing it, Chanel is doing it. But UOOYAA doesn't carry the baggage of luxury brands, so it can be more adventurous, freer, less like "brand content." It grows freely into a brand narrative publication — between a brand lookbook, an art publication and a cultural magazine, but its center of gravity always leans toward building the spiritual world. In the Chinese market, this is one of the few complete examples. In this era, someone is still persisting in doing this. Rare.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-372413d86e6efacbfea0e9b75fbeea84_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

## The Meaning of Print Media in This Era

The texture of UOOYAA ZINE surprised me. You can re-experience the charm of paper magazines. The temperature of paper, the smell of ink, the rhythm of turning pages — details that screens cannot replicate, settling the brand's avant-garde aesthetic and global vision into a **collectible cultural asset.**

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-5ef7ac68be447977904a4e2b69b0e782_1440w.gif?source=ccfced1a)

The UOOYAA in my hands

This magazine is a photography & art album. The cover is hardbound; every page is thick card stock, wonderful to touch. How much do I love albums?

During a trip to Greece, while friends went shopping, I found a very thick album in an art bookstore — extremely heavy. I carried it all the way back to Beijing on the plane. A senior companion said, this is true love.

![img](https://pica.zhimg.com/80/v2-e477b73bf3f1e89cade501380982c4d1_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

Reading a magazine and scrolling a feed feel completely different. Scrolling a feed makes you anxious.

Reading UOOYAA ZINE makes you slow down. **Like looking at a photo album.** **You can feel the accumulation of fashion design, photography, illustration, installation art and other artistic languages combined.** Some art projects specifically deconstruct and reshape everyday things — familiar objects recombined into completely unexpected works.

Humor, absurdity, wild imagination — injecting new life and playfulness into daily life.

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-f9ecb0d5e6595ad72ef342455bbad922_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

## The Cosmic Editorial Department

Opening this magazine feels like watching an exhibition, but you don't have to follow the guide route — you are free. **It's like a tunnel suddenly appears before your eyes. This tunnel opens, letting you travel between past, present and future, shuttling between dreams and reality.**

![img](https://picx.zhimg.com/80/v2-827f1d0e4911525c006c0bfe7060de51_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

From a routinely operating system of discipline, find an exit for yourself. **Briefly lose contact there, imagine freely, not captured by any identity.** Like the description of Issue 6 — new humans live here, individual, rational, humorous, sharp. The future here has less metallic coldness and more warmth of everyday life.

![img](https://pic1.zhimg.com/80/v2-5731c93f0b6bbd93ccf5c5c05d5bec7f_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

UOOYAA ZINE feels especially precious today. It doesn't need to prove itself loudly. Every page is quiet, polished frame by frame by the editors — meticulous editorial awareness and sustained aesthetic patience, **building a journey of reading and imagination full of playful surprises.**

![img](https://pic1.zhimg.com/80/v2-a6a0ccf7dd78b514d4de9fc027ccb6b6_1440w.jpg?source=ccfced1a)

This magazine lets you reconnect with a long-lost free and alive feeling. Go see the summer muse girls. You'll remember you were once like that too — spirit fully released, experiencing the world truthfully and joyfully.
