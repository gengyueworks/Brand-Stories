***What is***

***Radio Revival Festival?***

It was never meant to be a conventional event.

It is perpetually whimsical.

**Radio Revival Festival** is a celebration created by Mao Wang Radio.

The crazy part? Last year they held it in the Sahara Desert in Morocco, where they burned a pirate ship. This year, they came to the desert beside Mingsha Mountain in Dunhuang.

The Craftsmanship

and Youthful Spirit

of Mr. Zeng

Mao Wang Radio's founder, Mr. Zeng Dejun — we genuinely admire and adore him.

**Mr. Zeng is in his sixties, a senior expert, a master craftsman,** yet he carries a kind of adolescent, youthful spirit.

So many things he said on site made me applaud in my heart.

When he came over to clink glasses with us warmly, we said, "Mr. Zeng, we're cheering you on like crazy." Monica asked with concern, "Sir, do you know what 'da call' means?" His adorable, mischievous expression immediately surfaced. He said, "Hey, if I didn't know that, I'd be out of touch~"

Haha.

I watched every video of Mr. Zeng I could find online. 👇

He is not only a radio enthusiast and a veteran of the audio industry — the designer of China's first commercial tube amplifier — but also a dreamer.

![Mr. Zeng Dejun](images/033.jpg)

Ever since he first saw a radio at the age of six, his destiny has been intertwined with this rectangular box. For a lifetime, he has been obsessed with and devoted to radios, audio equipment, and music.

He has handcrafted many radios, year after year, tinkering and experimenting.

**"You might ask if it's meaningful. I don't know either. But I think — this is what I love."**

![Mr. Zeng Dejun at work](images/034.jpg)

**"For these thirty years, I have only done what I love. And perfected it to the extreme. This is my life."**

"Being a craftsman is so lonely!"

"Having dealt with sound my whole life, in these ten years of building Mao Wang, I've wanted to give up countless times. But I couldn't bear to let it go. Sometimes, I was truly the only one persevering. No one around me understood why I was doing something that didn't make money!"

But "**a person should have this kind of faith in their passion, in their interests. To persist. To give.**"

***Why Did I Cry***

***at the Product Launch?***

![At the product launch event](images/035.jpg)

I sat at the product launch event completely unprepared.

Before this, I had never sat through an entire product launch. At most, I'd listen to half and then get up to take photos.

But this time, I sat for over three hours and listened from beginning to end. I nearly cried. In the end, I was moved to stand up and applaud.

Because I felt something deeply warm. I understood that this was an incredibly lovely team.

Later, I heard them say that for months, the team had slept only two hours a day for this event. They truly gritted their teeth and worked hard, crafting products with genuine passion, and won readers who truly love this brand.

![At the launch event](images/036.jpg)

Their sincerity is reflected in every detail.

After the launch, when we returned to our hotel, two new products were placed on every bedside. To ensure the music listening experience, each room was also equipped with a dedicated network stabilizer — so you could connect and listen to music smoothly.

![New products in the hotel room](images/037.jpg)

My new roommate gleefully picked up the radio and went to the desert to listen to music and watch the stars.

And then I started fervently recommending Mao Wang to friends and readers.

**The Title**

While writing this essay, I connected to OhPlay on my phone and listened to the radio as I wrote.

One morning, Ma Shifang played an hour of Bob Dylan. My happiness overflowed.

I remembered many years ago, when I had just joined the magazine, Mr. Ma Shifang wrote an article for *Lens Visual* titled "Why Dylan Is Cool." I couldn't put it down and read it over and over.

Later, when Bob Dylan came to perform at Beijing Workers' Stadium, I went to the concert.
