Hi, I'm Yue. I'm back in Beijing from my travels.

Whenever I write about the brilliance of New Yorkers, the tattoo artists of Berlin, the seven-day wonder of Burning Man, the Radio Revival Festival, fine wine, fine food, Beijing's cafes, the poetry of the city... these stories,

**"If, in a lifetime, you can encounter a person or a moment that gives you the feeling of 'escaping mediocrity' — what an honor."**

Life has infinite possibilities.

Interesting people

Those moments when your body and soul connect brilliantly with the world. Those moments when you see a door to a tiny universe swing open for you...

You and I need moments like these in our lives. When you feel a breadth of vision and a quality of texture you've never sensed before. It makes the road ahead more meaningful. More purposeful.

That time I lost my passport in San Francisco — I was truly wrecked.

A local friend took me to a brewery full of stories. As I tasted coffee-flavored, cherry-flavored, and other craft beers, each one bearing a name like a film title —

Before my eyes: laughter and chatter, friends gathered in twos and threes at tables, people with their kids and pets, eating, drinking, chatting, playing pool. The atmosphere was relaxed.

The photos above are the ones I took that day.

**🍺**

I immersed myself in that absolutely beautiful moment. Rich fragrance on my tongue, and for a while, my worries were set aside.

I laughed until I doubled over. **The white foam gently painted my friend's upper lip and mine, like a stroke of a mustache.**

My inner energy quietly returned: **those troubles** can be solved. And the beautiful things are far more worth my energy to create.

**Back in Beijing, there's one incredibly cool place for craft beer — Jing-A!**

If you ever get the chance to drink the rich, impossibly fresh beer they draw straight from the equipment in their brewhouse — it's a **sensory explosion!**

![Jing-A brewing equipment](images/011.jpg)

Jing-A is fiery, cool, and knows how to have fun. It was founded in 2012 by two longtime friends, Alex and Kris.

They love Beijing and draw inspiration from this city, **brewing beers that can never settle for mediocrity. Always creating surprises.**

They are **obsessed with finding interesting ingredients and creating flexible, free-spirited flavors**: sour plum soup (a new brew), Sichuan peppercorn, osmanthus flower, Xinjiang raspberry, Japanese sake rice, roasted sweet potato, and watermelon — all flavors they've developed.

I love the curiosity, honesty, and sincerity that flows and collides so freely. **Never setting limits**, unleashing imagination, **permanently changing the beer tastes of Chinese beer lovers.**

![Jing-A brand](images/012.jpg)

To be able to drink the fine beer of one of China's first craft beer brands — it's a blessing.

They're not just cool. They have **real chops: ✨ the highest-rated brewery in China on the Untappd craft beer rating app, winner of multiple global craft beer medals across categories.**

✨ **Winner of "Best Small Brewery in Asia" at the Asia Beer Awards.**

![Jing-A beer animation](images/013.gif)

![Jing-A beer](images/014.jpg)

And they really know how to have fun! The craft beer festival that Jing-A has initiated and hosted in recent years is a **cross-cultural brewing extravaganza** that many friends in Beijing eagerly anticipate every year.

Each year, the **8x8 Brewing Collaboration Project** invites

**8 breweries from Greater China × 8 breweries from a selected region around the world**

Sixteen breweries, randomly paired, collaborate to brew **8 phenomenal beers for the 8x8 Collaboration Project**,

unveiled at **the beer festival held every mid-October!**

![8x8 beer festival animation](images/015.gif)

In 2017, they invited breweries from the U.S. West Coast — the "birthplace of craft beer."

In 2018, they turned to **Nordic** countries — "the highest standard of craft beer today."

And in **2019**, the invited overseas craft breweries **all come from the U.S. East Coast and Canada — "the frontiers of craft beer innovation"!**

As a storyteller, I've been deeply captivated and fascinated by the **stories** of these craft breweries.

The founding visions, the devotion to craft, the sparks of inspiration and creativity, the freedom of expression — it's electrifying to read about!

**E.T. Brewery (from Shenzhen) ×**

**Graft Cider (from Newburgh, New York)**

![E.T. beer](images/017.jpg)

**E.T.** was founded in 2016. Their beer names all carry an **extraterrestrial flair** —

from **Angry Alien** — a classic Double IPA — to more experimental brews like **Anywhere Door** — a dark sour IPA.

![E.T. beer](images/018.jpg)

![E.T. beer](images/019.jpg)

**Graft Cider** is a modern sour cider company based in Newburgh, New York.

Their ciders are brewed from **fresh apples from the Hudson Valley**, with styles ranging from slightly tart, **adventurous flavors** to wild, modern expressions of the cider tradition.

Across the four sessions of this year's 8x8,

eight different ciders will be served.

Two from the "Cloud City" series, with **lactose, fruit, and hops** added — **flavors reminiscent of a milkshake IPA. "Salt and Sand,"** a Gose-style cider inspired by the Margarita, with **rich citrus and smoky notes.**

![Graft Cider](images/020.jpg)

**Heroes Beer Co. (from Hong Kong) × Finback Brewery (from Queens, New York)**

![Heroes × Finback](images/021.jpg)

**Heroes Beer Co.** names every beer after **local people they admire:** a radio DJ who loves pairing beer recommendations with songs, a renowned local chef...

They collaborate with these **"heroes" of everyday life**, **capturing each person's unique character** to craft the **most distinctive** beers.

![Heroes beer](images/022.jpg)

**Finback** comes from Queens, New York. Even though they are now one of the best breweries in town, they continue to release fresh beer in limited-edition cans and draft at their own taproom.

This preserves the **freshness of flavor** to the greatest extent and allows everyone **to flexibly try a wide variety of different beer styles.**

![Finback beer](images/023.jpg)

At this year's 8x8 Collaboration Project,

**Heroes Beer Co. brings:**

Spilly Berry — a sweet fruit beer brewed with **strawberry, raspberry, guava,** and **boysenberry.**

Hangry Donut — a wheat beer brewed with **lactose, hibiscus flower,** and a variety of **citrus hops.**

**Finback Brewery brings:**

Haramre — an Imperial Stout with **roasted coconut aroma.**

Aqua Dementia — their collaboration with To Øl, a **hazy Double IPA**

(they participated in last year's collaboration project)!

All of these will be brought to Beijing, waiting for everyone to taste.

**Stillwater Artisanal (Brooklyn, New York)**

**Stillwater Artisanal** was founded by a former internationally renowned radio DJ who, through his own **creativity and skill**, has brewed some of the world's most unique and highly rated beers.

This brand is also a **groundbreaking art project** he collaborates on with friends.

/ Has collaborated with graphic designers, infusing **pop culture, politics, and social commentary** into the brand's visual identity.

**Bravo Brewing (from Guangzhou) × Lamplighter Brewing Co. (from Cambridge, Massachusetts)**

![Bravo × Lamplighter](images/033.jpg)

Bravo Brewing was founded in Guangzhou in August 2016.

The founders and brewers were deeply moved by the **independent and free spirit of craft beer culture** and, upon returning to China, ignited a **new wave** in craft brewing.

They not only select premium ingredients to brew classic ales and lagers but also continuously innovate with more distinctly **local** brews.

![Bravo beer](images/034.jpg)

**Lamplighter** focuses on New England IPAs with an emphasis on **aroma and flavor**, barrel-aged sour ales, and seasonal specials.

They brew one-off batches, **infusing them with our whimsical ideas**, never shying away from reimagining and extending traditional thinking and unique ingredients.

**At this year's 8x8, Bravo Brewing brings:**

Infinite Guide Gose — a double champion that won both the Gold Medal and Best European Malt Beer at this year's Australian International Beer Festival.

We're also looking forward to Bravo's collaboration brew with NBeer Pub — Chicken Abalone Saison.

Lamplighter brings 8 beers.

The one I'm most excited about is **Cherry Riley** — a fermented sour ale aged for two years in **Hungarian Chardonnay barrels**, with **cherries** added in the final two months.

They'll also be serving **Group Theory** — an **American Wheat** brewed with **local fresh honey and chamomile flowers**, smooth and relaxing.

**Yun Brewing (from Kunming) × Thin Man Brewery (from Buffalo, New York)**

![Yun Brewing × Thin Man](images/036.jpg)

![Yun Brewing](images/037.jpg)

**Thin Man Brewery** named themselves after the **crash test dummy.**

![Yun Brewing animation](images/038.gif)

Yun Brewing was founded in Kunming by a group of close friends passionate about their craft.

Surrounded by Yunnan's uniquely blessed natural environment and cuisine, they've experimented with many one-of-a-kind combinations of craft beer and classic Yunnan elements — **sticky rice Porter, Schisandra Saison, and Wild Rose Ale.**

At this year's 8x8, Thin Man's head brewer Ruby Watkins presents **Sauvage Alliance** — an **American Wild Ale** co-brewed with New Belgium Brewing of Colorado, rested in aged barrels with Cabernet Sauvignon juice.

**Glow Worm** — a **Gose Sour** at 4.4% ABV, with added **guava** and **blackberry.**

A beer named after the festival itself: **Willie Nelson Saaz Hello** — a **Wild Saison** dry-hopped with Nelson and **Saaz hops.** Yun Brewing will bring even more creative beers!

I'm truly excited to see the sparks fly between these two imaginative breweries!

**Nanmen (from Chengdu) × Collective Arts Brewing (from Canada)**

![Nanmen × Collective Arts](images/040.jpg)

Nanmen Brewing was born in Chengdu, dedicated to capturing the fun and flavor of Chengdu's food culture.

They are experimenting with local fresh fruits, herbs, locally sourced microorganisms, and more creative combinations.

![Nanmen beer](images/041.jpg)

![Nanmen beer](images/042.jpg)

**Collective Arts** is a grassroots brand that fuses **the creativity of craft beer** with **the inspiration of emerging artists and musicians.**

They print limited-edition artist works on their beer cans and labels, ensuring that the beer inside the can is as **diverse and creative** as the artists they feature.

If you ever travel to Hamilton, Ontario, don't miss their **Bottle Gallery**, which showcases the works of the artists they've collaborated with.

At this year's 8x8 Collaboration Project,

**Collective Arts brings Guava Gose**

and their collaboration with Sweden's Stigbergets Brewery (from last year's 8x8) —

a **New-Style India Pale Lager!**

**Nanmen** will bring even more creative product combinations!

**Taihu Brewing (from Taiwan, China) × Brooklyn Brewery (from Brooklyn, New York)**

![Taihu × Brooklyn Brewery](images/044.jpg)

Taihu, thriving in Taipei, has now established craft beer bars in Xiamen and Chengdu, and has opened a pop-up shop in Beijing. Looking forward to their hop-forward IPAs and more experimental new brews.

![Brooklyn Brewery](images/045.jpg)

Jing-A once went to New York to explore the story of **Brooklyn Brewery's craft beer adventure.**

They discovered a mature brewery hidden in one of the most expensive real estate districts in the world.

Master brewer Garrett Oliver oversees the operation here,

including the brewing of extraordinary barrel-aged sour ales, Imperial Stouts, and other experimental beers.

At this year's 8x8 Collaboration Project,

**Taihu brings a Black Tea Amber Ale** — just hearing the name makes your heart stir.

**Brooklyn Brewery brings** many beers brewed and sold only in the New York market,

and rare offerings like **Ghost Bottle**, specially brewed for events like 8x8!
