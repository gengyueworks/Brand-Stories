### BUTEA

DAYDREAM Limited Edition Flower & Fruit Tea

![Daydream tea](images/005.jpg)

![Daydream tea](images/006.jpg)

**Daydream** — that is the theme of this fruit tea. I opened this white box at noon in Jerusalem.

The brand's founder is my friend, and also a devoted, longtime reader of JUSTGO. When I unwrapped the package and saw the word DAYDREAM, I understood instantly. **A soul-level understanding.**

![Tea bags](images/007.jpg)

Every flavor is inspired by a daydream set free. Warm, sweet, wild, untethered. They lift you away from the unease, anxiety, and dullness of the present — to islands, forests, spring light, summer days — letting you feel the leisure and ease life should hold, and drift into a long, pleasant daydream.

![Brewing tea](images/008.gif)

As a sensualist, I quickly fell in love with the whole experience — opening each sachet, steeping, and sipping.

Japanese Oolong: a hidden white peach within deep tea fragrance. Apple Tree Summer: the rich scent of branches scorched by a midsummer afternoon sun, mingled with crisp apple. Sweetheart Red Berry: raspberry wrapped in tender rose. Asakusa Lime: fresh and faintly tart, a curious modulation, a sip of afternoon sea breeze slipping between the lips.

![Tea](images/009.jpg)

On my travels and creative projects this year, the inspiration of nature's blossoms and fruits has accompanied me every time.

"Infuse freshness and surprise into every sachet. Let every sip be a **unique, sensory experience that delights body and mind.**"

If you're thinking of trying tea, I recommend it. Only surprises, no disappointment. Enjoy.

### Duolingo

Language learning app (Free)

![Duolingo](images/011.jpg)

Duolingo is the world's coolest language learning platform. The #1 most downloaded language learning app globally, with 300 million users.

I went to film their first-ever offline party in China. The founder, Luis von Ahn, flew to Beijing to meet Chinese users. Even though my workload was exploding at the time, my old friend Haina invited me (we were close partners back in the Airbnb days), so I went without a second thought — out of loyalty.

I was actually an early Duolingo user myself, but the stories and passion of the power users at the event truly moved and inspired me. Some had **studied for six consecutive years, never missing a single daily check-in!** There was also a mom who picked up French, passed all the levels, and now shares a deeper language with her son — making her retirement life endlessly joyful.

The atmosphere that day was electric — a group of curious, creative, broad-minded, growth-oriented people, warm and witty and sharp. I really liked them and felt genuinely happy for them.

Recommended for anyone serious about learning a language.

### Kitchen Safe

Phone addiction timer lock box

![Kitchen Safe](images/013.jpg)

![Kitchen Safe](images/014.jpg)

This one is brilliant. **The ultimate internet detox tool.**

Originally designed abroad as a "kitchen safe" to keep high-calorie snacks out of reach, it later proved equally effective for **locking away phones**, game consoles, and more.

Put something inside, set the timer, and it absolutely cannot be opened until the countdown ends. Then you can actually get work done.

Perfect for freelancers. If you're a chronic procrastinator or want to **block out phone distractions**, take a look. Even if you're naturally focused and resistant to distraction — I've experienced it firsthand — locking your phone away for a stretch lets you sink into **pure creative flow for three to four hours. Your productivity can triple, easily.**
