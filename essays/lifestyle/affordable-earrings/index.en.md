### A Few Reminders

- Earrings are **deeply personal**. If you can't wear them, don't force it.
- **You get what you pay for.** Although the descriptions say hypoallergenic, if your **ears are sensitive**, it's best to **buy based on your own experience and body**.
- Beyond Taobao's convenience, you can also try on earrings at **offline fashion boutiques** — finding what suits your face shape is even better.
- My **earring order**: I pick the outfit first, do my makeup, and the final step is choosing earrings — the finishing touch.
- I only wear earrings when I go out to shoot, travel, or meet people. Not in daily life.
- Those thinking of **piercing their ears** — find a proper, reputable place. Above all, avoid infection and pus, especially in summer.
- If you don't have pierced ears, don't worry — many of the styles here have **clip-on alternatives**. (Though I've always found clip-ons a bit painful; I can manage about two to three hours before needing a break.)
